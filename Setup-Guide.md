# Import
<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c17f5a0-15f3-4396-a277-a66638017fb3"> </p> 

Click on `Import` in [streamer.bot](https://streamer.bot/) and drag the [BlackjackGame.sb](https://github.com/aaskjer/Blackjack-Game/releases) file into the window or copy paste the [import string](https://github.com/aaskjer/Blackjack-Game/blob/main/Import-String.md).

<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a1c731fd-6ef0-4bf2-a312-05e3c95e0feb"> </p> 

Click `Import` and `Yes` on the next popups to import Blackjack Game

<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/19cc11ee-647e-401a-aafd-cde610918e6b"> </p>

Enable all commands in the `Commands` tab of streamer.bot.

# Setup

<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8b7c43b3-d9de-4518-94a5-e9d38c8e219d"> </p>

Right-Click on `Test` and click `Test Trigger` in the trigger section to open the GUI of the game.

<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1c7f7cdb-5fdd-4fb6-b802-f3eb6aedea48"> </p>

Setup everything you like, hit save and have fun!

# OBS Overlay

<p align="center"><img alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/80f527c8-6156-430b-98cc-3e9d9bfed429"> </p>

Connect streamer.bot with OBS, adjust everything you need and hit the button `Setup Overlay in OBS`. The Game will create everything in OBS for you to tinker further with it.
